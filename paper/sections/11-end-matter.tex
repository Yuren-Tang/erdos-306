\section*{Formalization and code availability}

A machine-checked Lean~4 formalization of Erd\H{o}s Problem~306 is archived on
Zenodo \cite{TangErdos306}.  The formalization encodes the finite set of
denominators as a finite strictly increasing tuple; this is a representational
detail and does not strengthen the statement of Theorem~\ref{thm:headline}.
The article and the formalization use different analytic presentations.  The
article derives the required dyadic estimates from the prime number theorem,
whereas the archived formalization uses explicit estimates of Rosser and
Schoenfeld \cite{RosserSchoenfeld1962}.  The archive provides supporting code
and an independently checkable record of the theorem; it is not used in place
of the mathematical arguments above.  The formalization was developed in
connection with the Formal Conjectures benchmark \cite{FormalConjectures2026}.
