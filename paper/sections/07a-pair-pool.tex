\section{Reciprocal mass, weights, and variance}\label{sec:mass}

Let
\[
 \Pk(k_0)=\bigcup_{k=k_0}^{3k_0}\Pk_k,
 \qquad
 S_1=\sum_{p\in\Pk(k_0)}\frac1p,
 \qquad
 S_2=\sum_{p\in\Pk(k_0)}\frac1{p^2}.
\]
By \eqref{eq:inclusive-window}, this is the prime interval
$[2^{k_0},2^{3k_0+1})$.  Expanding $S_1^2$ gives the exact identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pair-pool}
 \sum_{\substack{p,q\in\Pk(k_0)\\p<q}}\frac1{pq}
 =\frac{S_1^2-S_2}{2}.
\end{equation}
The analytic mass input gives $S_1\ge21/20$, and
\[
 S_2\le\sum_{n\ge2^{k_0}}\frac1{n^2}
 \le\frac1{2^{k_0}-1}.
\]
For $k_0\ge5$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pool-half}
 \sum_{p<q}\frac1{pq}\ge\frac12.
\end{equation}
The products are distinct squarefree semiprimes by unique factorization.

Before choosing the mass batch, fix the control edges and a finite gadget edge
set $\Egad$ to be defined in Section~\ref{sec:minor}.  Since the number of
gadget edges is fixed before $k_0$ and their second prime lies in a high block,
their reciprocal load tends to zero.  Proposition~\ref{prop:control-estimates}
therefore gives, at the final scale,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:base-load}
 \Lambda_{\mathrm{base}}:=
 \sum_{e\in\Ectrl\dot\cup\Egad}\frac1e
 <\frac{3}{2b}.
\end{equation}
Set $t=3/(2b)-\Lambda_{\mathrm{base}}>0$.

The pair pool excludes three types of products: the finite obstruction $T$,
control products, and gadget products.  At large scale, every member of $T$ is
below the bottom pair scale, so it removes nothing.  The reciprocal load lost
to control and gadget intersections is at most
$\Lambda_{\mathrm{base}}$.  Hence
\[
 t+\operatorname{load}(\text{forbidden pool terms})
 \le\frac{3}{2b}\le\frac12,
\]
and \eqref{eq:pool-half} shows that the allowed residual pool has load at least
$t$.  This is an exact cancellation; no surplus beyond the lower bound $1/2$
is used.
