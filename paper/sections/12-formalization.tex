\section{Formalization and code availability}\label{sec:formalization}

A machine-checked Lean~4 formalization of Erd\H{o}s Problem~306 is archived on
Zenodo \cite{TangErdos306}.  The article and the formalization present the
analytic input differently: the article uses the prime number theorem and
proves the local reciprocal-prime law in Proposition~\ref{prop:local-law},
whereas the archived formalization uses explicit estimates of
Rosser--Schoenfeld \cite{RosserSchoenfeld1962}.  The formal archive is supporting
code and an independently checkable record of the theorem; it is not used as a
substitute for the mathematical arguments given above.
