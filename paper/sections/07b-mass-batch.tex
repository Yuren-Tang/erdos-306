\begin{lemma}[Greedy reciprocal window]\label{lem:greedy}
Let $(x_i)$ be a finite family of positive reals of total at least $t$, and
suppose $x_i<g$ for every $i$.  Some subfamily has sum in $[t,t+g)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose a subfamily of minimal cardinality among those with sum at least $t$.
Removing any selected term drops the sum below $t$, so the original sum is
less than $t+x_i<t+g$.
\end{proof}

Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:greedy} with $x_e=1/e$, $g=3/(2b)$, and the residual
target $t$.  The bottom pair scale ensures every $1/e<g$.  We obtain a mass
batch $\Emass$ such that, for
\[
 E=\Ectrl\dot\cup\Egad\dot\cup\Emass,
 \qquad
 \Lambda=\sum_{e\in E}\frac1e,
\]
\begin{equation}\label{eq:load-window}
 \frac{3}{2b}\le\Lambda<\frac3b.
\end{equation}
Every denominator is a distinct squarefree semiprime, avoids $T$, and divides
\begin{equation}\label{eq:period}
 L=b\prod_{p\in\Bset}p.
\end{equation}
Define the uniform weight
\begin{equation}\label{eq:theta}
 \theta=\frac{1/b}{\Lambda}.
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:theta-window}
 \frac13<\theta\le\frac23,
 \qquad
 \sum_{e\in E}\frac\theta e=\frac1b,
 \qquad
 \Lambda<1.
\end{equation}
The middle identity is the exact cancellation of the main-arc linear term, and
the last inequality gives \eqref{eq:no-wrap}.
