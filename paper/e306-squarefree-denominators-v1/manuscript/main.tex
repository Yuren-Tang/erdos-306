\begin{abstract}
We characterize the positive rational numbers that are finite sums of reciprocals of distinct squarefree semiprimes. Such a representation exists if and only if the denominator in lowest terms is squarefree; moreover, the representing denominators may avoid any prescribed finite set. For a reduced target $t=a/b\in(0,1)$ with squarefree $b$, we construct an explicit family consisting of products of two primes in $[X,X^\gamma)$ together with auxiliary rows indexed by the prime divisors of $b$. Finite Fourier inversion reduces the problem to positivity of one coefficient. An upper fixed-ratio block of primes synchronizes the relevant Chinese-remainder coordinates, row estimates recover the remaining coordinates, and retained prime-pair factors control the noncentral frequencies. The central range has a positive Gaussian contribution, and a no-wrap argument converts the resulting congruence into exact equality. Prime dilution then gives the full characterization and finite avoidance.
\end{abstract}
