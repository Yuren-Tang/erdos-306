\begin{abstract}
We prove that a positive rational number is a finite sum of reciprocals of distinct integers, each the product of two distinct primes, if and only if its reduced denominator is squarefree; moreover, the representation may avoid any prescribed finite set of denominators.  The converse now follows directly from the fixed-target theorem by prime dilution.  The resulting representation fibres admit common refinements and countably infinite proper refinement chains, with local replacement distinguished from simultaneous globally proper replacement.  For a fixed reduced target $t=a/b\in(0,1)$ with squarefree $b$ and a fixed $\gamma>1$, we take all prime pairs from $[X,X^\gamma)$ together with target rows indexed by the prime-divisor set $S_b$.  The reciprocal load tends to $\lambda_\gamma=(\log\gamma)^2/2$, and the construction works in the sharp fixed-parameter region $t<\lambda_\gamma<1$.  Exact centring gives a Bernoulli parameter tending to $\alpha_{t,\gamma}=2t/(\log\gamma)^2$.  With $Z=X^\gamma$ and $\tau(b)=\sum_{r\in S_b}r^{-2}$, the total variance is asymptotic to $\alpha_{t,\gamma}(1-\alpha_{t,\gamma})\{(2X^2\log^2X)^{-1}+\tau(b)/(Z\log Z)\}$.  A universal cutoff $T_0=\kappa_0\min(X^2,Z)$ and a prime-only Gaussian transition give one exhaustive six-sector proof for every fixed $\gamma>1$, while the leading variance provider still changes at $\gamma=2$.  The fixed-target Fourier coefficient has the sharp Gaussian asymptotic.  Consequently the direct family has entropy rate $H(\alpha_{t,\gamma})$, with rate $\log2$ attained precisely for $0<t<1/2$ at $\gamma=\exp(2\sqrt t)$, and with the appropriate non-attained boundary suprema for $t\geq1/2$.  Exact-cardinality, Hamming-separation, balanced-relation, denominator-height, proper-refinement branching and global transfer consequences follow at the same restricted-family scale.
\end{abstract}
