\begin{abstract}
We characterize the positive rationals that are finite sums of reciprocals of distinct squarefree semiprimes. A reduced rational $a/b>0$ admits such a representation if and only if $b$ is squarefree; moreover, the representation may be chosen to avoid any prescribed finite set of denominators. This resolves Erd\H{o}s Problem 306.

For a fixed squarefree $b\ge3$ we construct a finite family $\mathcal E_X$ of squarefree semiprimes and place an independent Bernoulli law on its subsets, centred so that the expected reciprocal sum is $1/b$. Finite Fourier inversion, one-anchor rigidity, quantitative row observability, and retained damping show that the exact representation fibre has a local Gaussian profile. If $L_X$ is the natural common denominator, $\sigma_X^2$ is the reciprocal-sum variance, and $W_X(t)$ is the Bernoulli mass of exact representations of $t$ by the same family, then for every fixed $U>0$, uniformly for $|j|\le U L_X\sigma_X$,
\[
L_X\sigma_X W_X\!\left(\frac1b+\frac{j}{L_X}\right)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}
\exp\!\left(-\frac{j^2}{2L_X^2\sigma_X^2}\right)+o_U(1).
\]
In particular, the target $1/b$ has the expected local-limit mass. This yields exponentially many exact representations, with the ambient entropy exponent in a mesoscopic typical-cardinality window, and shows that one fixed family realizes a growing consecutive block of nearby lattice targets.
\end{abstract}
