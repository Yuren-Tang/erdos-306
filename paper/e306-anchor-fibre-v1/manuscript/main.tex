\begin{abstract}
We characterize the positive rationals that are finite sums of reciprocals of distinct squarefree semiprimes: they are exactly those with squarefree reduced denominator, and any prescribed finite set of denominators may be avoided.  For a fixed reduced target $t=a/b\in(0,1)$ and fixed $\gamma>1$, the present centred no-wrap construction works in the exact admissible range $t<(\log\gamma)^2/2<1$; one anchor block, finite Fourier inversion, and decoded prime and target-coordinate rows produce an exact representation.  The fixed-target coefficient has a sharp Gaussian asymptotic governed by the full variance, and within the direct family this yields entropy-rate multiplicity, exact-cardinality and Hamming-separated subfamilies.  Finite prescription also gives common refinements, countably infinite proper refinement chains, and quantitative transfer to every admissible rational.
\end{abstract}
