\title{Reciprocal Representations by Distinct Squarefree Semiprimes\\
\large An Anchor--Fibre Fourier Method}
\author{Yuren Tang}
\date{}

\begin{document}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We prove that a positive rational number is a finite sum of reciprocals of distinct integers, each the product of two distinct primes, if and only if its reduced denominator is squarefree.  The affirmative direction is obtained from an exact finite Fourier selection argument built around a single prime anchor block.  The proof separates four mechanisms: complete-family reciprocal capacity, recovery of a coherent integer label from local residue data, sharp weighted compression of product fibres to a decoded skeleton, and frequency-dependent damping on that skeleton.  We isolate the reusable deterministic ingredients, including an optimal weighted product-fibre inequality and a finite-group syndrome-separation principle, before verifying their arithmetic providers for the semiprime denominator system.  Positivity is first obtained in a finite quotient; a strict no-wrap argument then yields the required exact rational identity.
\end{abstract}
