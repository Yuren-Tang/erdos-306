\begin{abstract}
A positive rational is a finite sum of reciprocals of distinct squarefree semiprimes if and only if its reduced denominator is squarefree; the representing denominators may moreover avoid any prescribed finite set.  For a fixed reduced target $t=a/b\in(0,1)$ and a fixed $\gamma>1$ with $t<(\log\gamma)^2/2<1$, we give a direct construction from primes in $[X,X^\gamma)$.  Finite Fourier inversion encodes the target, an anchor block synchronizes low-energy Chinese-remainder coordinates, and anchor-cross rows decode the remaining prime and target coordinates.  Exact weighted fibre compression then replaces each fibre by its decoded tuple while retaining every unassigned factor; in particular, the surviving lower--lower prime-pair factors provide the coercivity outside the major range.  The exact target coefficient has a sharp Gaussian asymptotic.  This yields entropy-rate multiplicity in the direct family, exact-cardinality and Hamming-separated subfamilies, quantitative proper-refinement branching, and global transfer to every rational with squarefree reduced denominator.
\end{abstract}
