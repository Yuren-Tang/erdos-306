\begin{abstract}
A positive rational is a finite sum of reciprocals of distinct squarefree semiprimes if and only if its reduced denominator is squarefree; the representing denominators may moreover avoid any prescribed finite set.  For a fixed reduced target $t=a/b\in(0,1)$ and a fixed $\gamma>1$ with $t<(\log\gamma)^2/2<1$, we construct one family from primes in $[X,X^\gamma)$.  The proof is organized through a source-twisted one-well composition interface: exact finite Fourier coefficients are combined with one-anchor observability, weighted fibre compression, an actual-kernel Gaussian well, source-uniform retained-factor tail bounds and a separate realization/no-wrap step.  This yields the exact fixed-target coefficient asymptotic and, uniformly on every fixed standardized compact window, a Gaussian moving-target profile for the same family.  Entropy-rate multiplicity, exact-cardinality and Hamming-separated subfamilies, proper-refinement branching and global transfer follow without introducing a second proof centre.
\end{abstract}
