\begin{abstract}
We characterize the positive rationals that are finite sums of reciprocals of distinct squarefree semiprimes. A reduced rational $a/b>0$ admits such a representation if and only if $b$ is squarefree; moreover, the representation may be chosen to avoid any prescribed finite set of denominators. This gives a self-contained analytic resolution of Erd\H{o}s Problem 306.

The analytic result is stronger. Fix a reduced proper rational $t=a/b\in(0,1)$ with squarefree $b$, and choose a fixed $\gamma>1$ such that
\[
t<\lambda_\gamma:=\frac{(\log\gamma)^2}{2}<1.
\]
With $Z=X^\gamma$, we use all prime pairs from $[X,Z)$ together with only polylogarithmically many target sensors for each prime dividing $b$, and place an independent Bernoulli law on the resulting family, centred exactly at $t$. If $L_X$ is the natural common denominator, $\sigma_X^2$ is the reciprocal-sum variance, and $W_X(s)$ denotes the Bernoulli mass of exact representations of $s$ by this fixed family, then for every fixed $U>0$, uniformly for $|j|\le U L_X\sigma_X$,
\[
L_X\sigma_X W_X\!\left(t+\frac{j}{L_X}\right)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}
\exp\!\left(-\frac{j^2}{2L_X^2\sigma_X^2}\right)+o_U(1).
\]
Thus the Bernoulli-weighted exact coefficients have a local Gaussian profile. The central coefficient yields exact representations, the same family realizes a growing consecutive block of nearby lattice targets, and the exact fibre contains exponentially many representations at the ambient entropy rate in a mesoscopic typical-cardinality window.
\end{abstract}
