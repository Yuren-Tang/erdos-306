\section{Discussion and conclusion}
\label{sec:scope}

The characterization and finite-avoidance theorem are global.  The coefficient asymptotic and entropy estimates belong to the explicit one-anchor family and to its open centred no-wrap range $t<\lambda_\gamma<1$; no impossibility statement is made beyond that range.

The construction isolates a reusable mechanism.  Fourier inversion selects the prescribed coefficient.  The anchor block turns many low-energy CRT coordinates into one integer label.  Anchor-cross rows recover the remaining prime coordinates and the coordinates carried by the reduced target denominator.  Exact weighted fibre compression retains, rather than averages away, every factor not assigned to a row.  In particular, the lower--lower prime-pair factors survive and control the transition and adaptive frequency ranges.  This retained-factor coercivity is what permits one six-sector proof on both sides of the variance boundary at $\gamma=2$.

The direct coefficient also separates qualitative existence from quantitative structure.  Its Gaussian asymptotic yields the exact entropy rate available in the chosen family, equal-cardinality extraction, Hamming separation and balanced reciprocal relations.  The replacement and transportation arguments then transfer this abundance to proper refinements and to arbitrary rationals with squarefree reduced denominator.  These conclusions preserve the distinction between a theorem about the constructed family and an optimization over all possible squarefree-semiprime representations.

Several natural variants remain open: suppressing nonzero aliases beyond no-wrap, proving a joint value--cardinality local limit, retaining target observability under structured deletions, and formulating an abstract anchored graph or hypergraph CRT local-limit theorem.  Effective numerical thresholds would require explicit prime-number-theorem errors and systematic tracking of the constants in the rigidity and sector estimates.

Thus the squarefree-denominator obstruction is exact.  The proof proceeds from one fixed Fourier coefficient to a synchronized CRT skeleton, and from that skeleton to a sharp local Gaussian law; prime dilution, replacement and transfer then supply the global theorem and its quantitative consequences.
