\section{The decoded skeleton and its minor ranges}
\label{sec:decoded-skeleton}

After \cref{prop:global-fibre-error}, it remains to estimate the single decoded tuple attached to each anchor assignment.  Coherent anchor assignments are indexed by an integer label $m$; noncoherent assignments are treated directly by the anchor partition.  The essential point is that the lower--lower pair factors were not discarded during fibre compression.

\subsection{Adaptive retained-pair damping}

For an integer $m$ with
\[
 X^2/4<|m|\leq M_{\mathrm{dec}},
\]
put
\[
 I_m=[2\sqrt{|m|},3\sqrt{|m|}].
\]

\begin{proposition}[Adaptive prime interval]
\label{prop:adaptive-prime-interval}
For every such $m$, the interval $I_m$ lies in $[X,Z/2)$ and contains
\[
 K_m\gg\frac{\sqrt{|m|}}{\log|m|}
\]
primes.  On the decoded skeleton, the retained complete-pair energy satisfies
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:adaptive-energy}
 Q_{\mathrm{pair}}(m)\gg\frac{|m|}{(\log|m|)^2}.
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Write $y=\sqrt{|m|}$.  The strict lower inequality gives $2y>X$.  At the upper endpoint,
\[
 3y\leq\frac{3\sqrt{XZ}}{\log Z}
 =\frac{3X^2}{\log Z}<Z/2
\]
for large $X$, using the definition of $M_{\mathrm{dec}}$.  Thus $I_m\subset P\setminus B$.  By \cref{lem:fixed-ratio-prime-supply},
\[
 K_m=\pi(3y)-\pi(2y)\gg y/\log y
\]
uniformly in the whole range.

For distinct primes $p,q\in I_m$,
\[
 4|m|\leq pq\leq9|m|,
\]
so
\[
 1/9\leq |m|/(pq)\leq1/4.
\]
This number already lies in $[0,1/2]$, and hence
\[
 \dist{m/(pq)}\geq1/9.
\]
By \cref{prop:prime-decoder-identification}, the $p$- and $q$-coordinates of the decoded skeleton equal $m$.  Every such $pq$ is a retained lower--lower denominator, so
\[
 Q_{\mathrm{pair}}(m)
 \gg\binom{K_m}{2}
 \gg K_m^2
 \gg\frac{|m|}{(\log|m|)^2}.
\]
The argument is unchanged for negative $m$, since circle distance is even.
\end{proof}

The adaptive estimate is summarized in \cref{prop:adaptive-prime-interval}.  The interval depends on $m$, but the estimate is pointwise at each decoded frequency; overlap between intervals belonging to different labels is irrelevant.

\subsection{The exhaustive frequency partition}

The squarefree period has the exact CRT decomposition
\[
 L=\prod_{r\in S_b}r\prod_{p\in P}p,
\]
and therefore
\[
 h\pmod L
 \longleftrightarrow
 \bigl((h\bmod q)_{q\in B},
       (h\bmod r)_{r\in R_{\mathrm{rows}}}\bigr).
\]
There is no extra lift multiplicity.

Fix a constant $C\geq1$, to be chosen before $X$, and put
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:N-major}
 N=\left\lfloor\frac{C}{\sigma_{\mathcal E}}\right\rfloor.
\end{equation}
For sufficiently large $X$, the definition \cref{eq:N-major} gives
\[
 N<X^2/4<M_{\mathrm{dec}},
\]
and the residues of the integers $|m|\leq N$ are distinct modulo $L$.

Every anchor assignment is either noncoherent, meaning $Q_B\geq F_B$, or coherent with a unique integer label $m$.  For a coherent assignment exactly one of the following disjoint ranges holds:
\begin{align*}
 \mathrm{I}.\quad &|m|\leq N,\\
 \mathrm{II}.\quad &N<|m|\leq X^2/4,\\
 \mathrm{III}.\quad &X^2/4<|m|\leq M_{\mathrm{dec}},\\
 \mathrm{IV}.\quad &|m|>M_{\mathrm{dec}}.
\end{align*}
For each anchor assignment, \cref{thm:weighted-compression} separates its single decoded tuple from all nondecoder tuples.  Thus every frequency belongs to exactly one of the following five sectors:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}c p{5.1cm} p{5.0cm}@{}}
\toprule
Sector & skeleton condition & control \\
\midrule
I & coherent, $|m|\leq N$ & positive Taylor major \\
II & coherent, $N<|m|\leq X^2/4$ & full-variance Gaussian tail \\
III & coherent, $X^2/4<|m|\leq M_{\mathrm{dec}}$ & adaptive retained pairs \\
IV & coherent, $|m|>M_{\mathrm{dec}}$ & internal anchor energy \\
V & noncoherent anchor assignments or nondecoder tuples & anchor entropy and fibre error \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{proposition}[Five-sector exhaustion]
\label{prop:five-sector-exhaustion}
The five sectors above are pairwise disjoint and exhaust all frequencies modulo $L$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The CRT coordinate map is a bijection.  The anchor assignment is either below or above the forcing floor.  Below the floor it has exactly one label by \cref{prop:exact-cold-rigidity}, and the four displayed label ranges are disjoint and exhaustive.  For each fixed anchor assignment, the row-coordinate product is partitioned into its single decoder tuple and the complementary tuples.  Hence every frequency occurs exactly once.
\end{proof}

The exhaustion statement \cref{prop:five-sector-exhaustion} leaves no residual sibling sector.  In Sectors I and II, \cref{prop:prime-decoder-identification,prop:target-decoder-identification} identify the complete coordinate tuple with the genuine integer $m$ modulo $L$.  In Sector III, only the $P$-coordinates are needed for \cref{eq:adaptive-energy}.  Sectors IV and V are controlled without any further decoder identification.

\subsection{Pointwise bounds on the minor skeleton ranges}

For a coherent label in Sector III, the retained pair factors and \cref{eq:bernoulli-modulus,eq:adaptive-energy} give
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sector-III-pointwise}
 |F(h_m)|
 \leq\exp\left(-c\frac{|m|}{(\log|m|)^2}\right).
\end{equation}
By \cref{eq:sector-III-pointwise}, throughout this range $\log|m|\asymp\log X$, so each term is at most $\exp(-cX^2/\log^2X)$.  Since there are $O(M_{\mathrm{dec}})=O(X^4)$ labels,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sector-III-total}
 \sum_{\mathrm{III}}|F(h_m)|
 \ll X^4\exp(-cX^2/\log^2X)
 =o(\sigma_{\mathcal E}^{-1}).
\end{equation}
No coordinate of $b$ is identified in this argument.

For a coherent label in Sector IV, the internal anchor factor and \cref{eq:top-exact-energy} give
\[
 |F(h_m)|\leq\exp(-\kappa_bm^2\sigma_{B,0}^2).
\]
The decoded row and residual factors have modulus at most one.  Hence
\begin{align}
\label{eq:sector-IV-total}
 \sum_{|m|>M_{\mathrm{dec}}}|F(h_m)|
 &\ll \sigma_{B,0}^{-1}
 \exp(-cM_{\mathrm{dec}}^2\sigma_{B,0}^2)\\
 &=o(\sigma_{\mathcal E}^{-1}),
\end{align}
because
\[
 M_{\mathrm{dec}}^2\sigma_{B,0}^2
 \gg\frac{X^2}{\log^6Z},
 \qquad
 \sigma_{B,0}^{-1}\ll Z\log Z.
\]

Finally, the noncoherent decoded-skeleton contribution is bounded by \cref{eq:noncoherent-top-tail}, while every nondecoder tuple is charged by \cref{eq:global-fibre-error}.  Therefore
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sector-V-total}
 \sum_{\mathrm{V}}|F|
 \ll \exp(-cF_B)
 +P_{\mathrm{anc}}(\exp(\Delta)-1)
 =o(\sigma_{\mathcal E}^{-1}).
\end{equation}

Sector II and the positive Sector I are treated together in the next section, because both use the actual-family variance rather than only the anchor or retained-pair energies.
