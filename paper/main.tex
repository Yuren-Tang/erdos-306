\begin{abstract}
We prove that a positive rational number is a finite sum of reciprocals of
 distinct squarefree semiprimes if and only if its reduced denominator is
 squarefree.  The prime number theorem, through a tail-uniform Abel-summation
 argument, supplies a common dyadic range containing both sufficiently many
 primes and reciprocal-prime mass exceeding one.  The remaining construction is
 finite.  Centred Chinese-remainder energies force low-energy residue
 assignments towards a global integer label; an entropy-sensitive level-set
 estimate and two tail bounds control the other assignments.  A separate pair
 family completes the reciprocal mass, while auxiliary semiprimes associated
 with the prime divisors of the target denominator suppress the residual
 Fourier frequencies.  A symbolic three-budget comparison then yields a
 positive finite Fourier count, and elementary avoidance arguments give the
 result for every positive rational with squarefree reduced denominator.
\end{abstract}
