## 1. Purpose

The seeded two-prime-per-side reduction fixes coprime squarefree prefixes `x,y`, a new left
prime `p`, and writes

```text
M_p=D(xp),
L_p=xpy-D(xp)D(y)>0,
K_p=x^2p^2L_p+y^2M_p^2.
```

A positive integral completion is equivalent to a divisor

```text
d | K_p,
d congruent -yM_p mod L_p.
```

The complementary divisor automatically has the same residue.  This note develops the exact
finite-group Fourier calculus for that prescribed-residue divisor problem and isolates three
strictly different levels:

```text
subgroup support,
bounded-exponent box support,
targeted Fourier cancellation/mixing.
```

## 2. Abstract square-root divisor problem

Let `L>=2`, let `K` be a positive integer coprime to `L`, and let `t` be a unit modulo `L`
satisfying

```text
K congruent t^2 mod L.                         (2.1)
```

Define

```text
N_t(K;L)=#{d:d|K, d congruent t mod L}.        (2.2)
```

For the E307 seed,

```text
L=L_p,
K=K_p,
t=-yM_p mod L_p.
```

Condition `(2.1)` follows from

```text
K_p congruent (yM_p)^2 mod L_p.
```

### Complement involution

The map

```text
d -> K/d                                         (2.3)
```

preserves the target divisor set, because

```text
K/d congruent t^2 t^(-1) congruent t mod L.
```

Hence target divisors occur in complementary pairs, except possibly for the fixed divisor
`sqrt(K)` when `K` is a square.

### Proposition 2.1 — parity

```text
N_t(K;L) is odd
```

if and only if `K` is a perfect square and

```text
sqrt(K) congruent t mod L.                     (2.4)
```

Otherwise `N_t(K;L)` is even.

For E307, a fixed complementary divisor gives `q=s`, so it cannot produce the required pair of
distinct primes.  Nondegenerate candidates therefore naturally occur as two-element orbits.

## 3. Group-algebra formulation

Write

```text
K=product_(i=1)^m lambda_i^(e_i),
G=(Z/LZ)^x,
g_i=lambda_i mod L.
```

Every divisor corresponds to an exponent vector

```text
f=(f_1,...,f_m), 0<=f_i<=e_i,
```

and has residue

```text
product_i g_i^(f_i).
```

In the integral group algebra `Z[G]`, put

```text
F_K=product_i(1+g_i+...+g_i^(e_i)).             (3.1)
```

### Theorem 3.1 — exact coefficient interpretation

```text
N_t(K;L)=[t]F_K.                                (3.2)
```

Thus the divisor-port problem is a bounded subset-product/code problem in the unit group.
The exponent box `0<=f_i<=e_i` is part of the arithmetic data and cannot in general be
replaced by the subgroup generated by the `g_i`.

## 4. Exact character formula

Let `Ghat` be the character group of `G`.  Character orthogonality gives:

### Theorem 4.1 — divisor-residue Fourier inversion

```text
N_t(K;L)
 =1/|G| sum_(chi in Ghat)
   conjugate(chi(t))
   product_i {1+chi(g_i)+...+chi(g_i)^(e_i)}.    (4.1)
```

Equivalently, if `D_K` is a uniformly random positive divisor of `K`, then

```text
Pr(D_K congruent t mod L)
 =1/|G| sum_chi conjugate(chi(t)) muhat_K(chi),  (4.2)
```

where

```text
muhat_K(chi)
 =product_i A_(e_i)(chi(g_i)),                  (4.3)
A_e(z)=(1+z+...+z^e)/(e+1).
```

The variables choosing the prime-power exponents independently and uniformly are the exact
probabilistic model.  This is a categorical/divisor analogue of the Bernoulli Fourier laws in
the E306 local-limit programme.

### Proposition 4.2 — centred terms are real

Under `(2.1)`, for every character `chi`,

```text
conjugate(chi(t)) sum_(d|K)chi(d)               (4.4)
```

is real.

#### Proof

Conjugation gives

```text
chi(t) sum_(d|K)chi(d)^(-1)
 =chi(t)/chi(K) sum_(d|K)chi(K/d)
 =conjugate(chi(t)) sum_(d|K)chi(d),
```

because `chi(K)=chi(t)^2` and complementary divisors permute the divisor set. `square`

Hence target existence is governed by a real signed character budget, not an arbitrary complex
sum.

### Exact targeted criterion

```text
N_t(K;L)>0
```

if and only if

```text
1+sum_(chi!=1)
  Re{conjugate(chi(t))muhat_K(chi)}>0.          (4.5)
```

A stronger sufficient condition is

```text
sum_(chi!=1)|muhat_K(chi)|<1.                  (4.6)
```

Condition `(4.6)` gives full near-uniform divisor-residue mixing and is often much stronger
than needed for a single target.  Formula `(4.5)` is the correct target-specific frontier.

## 5. Support subgroup and the quadratic certificate

Let

```text
H=<g_1,...,g_m> <=G.                            (5.1)
```

Every divisor residue lies in `H`.

### Theorem 5.1 — subgroup obstruction

If

```text
t notin H,                                     (5.2)
```

then `N_t(K;L)=0`.

Because `K congruent t^2` and `K` is a product of the `g_i`, one always has

```text
t^2 in H.                                      (5.3)
```

Therefore the coset `tH` has order at most two in `G/H`.

### Theorem 5.2 — every support obstruction has a quadratic character certificate

If `t notin H`, there exists a character `psi` of `G` such that

```text
psi(g_i)=1 for every i,
psi(t)=-1,
psi^2=1.                                       (5.4)
```

Consequently every divisor `d|K` satisfies `psi(d)=1`, whereas every integer congruent to `t`
has character value `-1`.

#### Proof

The nontrivial element `tH` has order two in the finite abelian quotient `G/H`.  A character of
that quotient separates it from the identity and may be chosen to take value `-1`.  Pull the
character back to `G`. `square`

This is the cheapest exact no-go test.  It is stronger and more structural than searching all
divisors.

### Local Legendre-symbol form

A common special case is a quadratic Dirichlet character.  If for some odd prime modulus
`ell|L` one has

```text
(lambda_i/ell)=+1 for every prime lambda_i|K,
(t/ell)=-1,
```

then no target divisor exists.

## 6. Why subgroup membership is not sufficient

Even if `t in H`, a representation

```text
t=product_i g_i^(n_i)
```

may require exponents outside the divisor box `0<=n_i<=e_i`.  Define the box support

```text
B_K={product_i g_i^(f_i):0<=f_i<=e_i}.          (6.1)
```

Then

```text
N_t(K;L)>0 iff t in B_K,                        (6.2)
```

where multiplicity in `B_K` is counted by `N_t`.

Thus the exact hierarchy is:

```text
t notin H        => quadratic support obstruction;
t in H\B_K       => bounded-exponent box obstruction;
t in B_K         => one or more complementary divisor ports.
```

The second layer is a finite knapsack/code-support problem, not detected by subgroup
characters that are trivial on `H`.

## 7. Collision and layer amplification

If the prime-power factors are partitioned into coprime blocks

```text
K=K_1...K_r,
```

then the uniform divisor law is a convolution

```text
mu_K=mu_(K_1)*...*mu_(K_r)                     (7.1)
```

on `G`, and Fourier coefficients multiply.

Parseval gives

```text
sum_chi |muhat_K(chi)|^2
 =|G| Pr(D_K congruent D'_K mod L),             (7.2)
```

where `D_K,D'_K` are independent uniform divisors.  Therefore the same mechanism developed
for hot sensor layers applies here:

```text
spectral radius
+ two-copy divisor collision entropy
+ convolution across independent factor blocks
=> residue flattening.
```

For squarefree `K`, if `chi(lambda_i)=exp(i theta_i)`, then

```text
|muhat_K(chi)|
 =product_i |cos(theta_i/2)|.                   (7.3)
```

A nontrivial character is strongly damped when many prime-factor residues are bounded away
from its kernel.

## 8. Computational probe

A Wolfram-language computation used:

- the first six primes to form ordered disjoint prefixes of sizes one or two;
- the first twelve primes as possible seeds `p`;
- every seed satisfying `L_p>1` and `gcd(L_p,yM_p)=1`.

It found `1457` admissible seeds.  Their exact target-divisor counts were:

```text
N_t=0 : 1452 seeds,
N_t=2 :    5 seeds.
```

All five positive cases were degenerate and gave no E307 prime completion.

For the `500` seeds with `L_p<=2000`, exact subgroup closure gave:

```text
t notin H, N_t=0 : 250,
t in H,    N_t=0 : 247,
t in H,    N_t=2 :   3.
```

This demonstrates that:

1. the quadratic subgroup obstruction already removes half the small sample;
2. bounded-exponent box support is a separate and comparably severe obstruction;
3. actual target divisors are extremely sparse before primality is tested.

These computations are diagnostic only and have no exclusion force for E307.
