
If a future E306 manuscript uses the one-anchor architecture, it should state

should not replace the E306 theorem as the public headline.

A separate paper is premature until there is a second substantive consumer.

## 2. What has genuine general value

The following results are independent of primes and semiprimes and are proved
in this packet.

### Weighted decoded-skeleton theorem

The exact tensorized error is

```text
sum_y W_y {
 product_r(alpha_(r,y)+beta_(r,y))
 -product_r alpha_(r,y)
}.
```

After decoder normalization this becomes the weighted average

```text
sum_y A_y {product_r(1+epsilon_(r,y))-1},
```

with `exp(Delta)-1` as a corollary.  This is a reusable deterministic theorem,
not an E306-specific estimate.

### Anchor–fibre Fourier transference

A positive exact Fourier coefficient follows from six independent providers:

1. weighted anchor partition;
2. shift-uniform fibre decoding;
3. uniform coherent-skeleton identification;
4. residual coherent-label damping;
5. positive complex major contribution;
6. no-wrap or exact alias removal.

The theorem applies to arbitrary finite abelian subset-sum systems.

### Quantitative target observability

Target sensitivity is a syndrome-map code-distance condition on a finite hidden
group.  Squarefree denominator rows are one application.

### Complete-family mass

For fixed uniformity `k`, elementary-symmetric capacity is controlled by the
first two power sums.  This is a clean mass theorem, explicitly separated from
rigidity.

## 3. What remains application-specific

The following are providers, not parts of the general transference proof:

- prime-block Gibbs or exact-cold partition estimates;
- multiplicity-sensitive inverse-residue packing;
- prime-inverse exponential-sum estimates;
- identification of CRT decoders with one integer label;
- complete-pair adaptive minor damping;
- the E306 Taylor variance and parameter ledger;
- squarefree-semiprime arithmetic closure.

The abstraction is valuable precisely because these can be replaced without
rewriting the transference theorem.

## 4. Mathematical gain per proof burden

### Gain

The abstraction:

- prevents the weighted-normalization error which motivated the workstream;
- identifies hidden target coordinates as an observability obligation;
- forces residual factors to remain on the decoded skeleton;
- separates major positivity from modulus estimates;
- gives a reusable theorem for future analytic sensor backends;
- clarifies which failures are structural and which are merely quantitative;
- shortens audit and proof-development interfaces.

### Burden

The core theorem and its proof are short.  Most of the seven-file packet is
explanation, counterexample, and provider mapping rather than new machinery.

The full generalization to arbitrary finite abelian targets and fixed
higher-uniformity mass is useful but would distract if inserted into the middle
of the E306 arithmetic proof.

### Verdict

The core proposition has high gain and low proof burden.  The surrounding
abstraction has moderate gain and should not dominate the first E306 paper.

## 5. Recommended manuscript architecture if one-anchor is selected

A future manuscript could use the following order.

### Main theorem and reduction

State the E306 result and the avoiding unit representation.

### Natural complete-family mass

Prove the pair identity and choose the Bernoulli parameter.

### Weighted anchor–fibre proposition

State a concise specialization of the general theorem:

```text
weighted anchor partition
+ summable row decoder tails
+ decoded-label identification
+ strict major/minor budget
=> positive exact coefficient.
```

Give its short deterministic proof.

### Arithmetic providers

Verify:

- anchor rigidity/partition;
- row-code separation;
- target observability;
- actual-family variance and major arc;
- coherent-label minor lanes;
- no-wrap.

### Appendix or final section

Record:

- the optimal tensorized theorem;
- general finite-group observability;
- complete `k`-family mass;
- failure modes.

This arrangement exposes the mechanism without making the paper read like an
abstract framework paper.

## 6. Why not option 1

Making the fully abstract theorem the public main theorem would overstate the
current publication dividend.

- The main mathematical audience initially needs the E306 arithmetic result.
- Most difficult providers remain arithmetic.
- There is not yet a second application demonstrating that the abstraction is
  the primary theorem rather than a powerful proof interface.

The general theorem should organize the proof, not displace its mathematical
subject.

## 7. Why not option 3 only

Putting the entire mechanism in an appendix would hide a load-bearing idea.
The weighted decoded-skeleton correction and retained residual damping explain
why the proof is valid.  A concise central proposition belongs in the main
argument if the route is used.

The broader target-group and higher-uniformity material may be appendicial, but
the core transference implication should not be.

## 8. Why not a separate paper yet

A separate mechanism paper becomes justified if at least one of the following
occurs:

1. a second non-semiprime application is completed;
2. the theorem is strengthened to exploit complex fibre cancellation rather
   than absolute compression;
3. a non-enumerative synchronization theorem crosses the current square-root
   method barrier;
4. the target-observability framework yields a classification beyond the
   elementary squarefree obstruction;
5. the transference theorem becomes a natural result in coding/Gibbs language
   with examples independent of E306.

At present, publication as a standalone paper would add exposition faster than
mathematical content.

## 9. Relation to later analytic improvements

The mechanism predicts how later improvements should be classified.

- Replacing elementary inverse-residue packing by Kloosterman estimates is a
  **technical provider upgrade**.
- Replacing explicit core enumeration by a spectral-profile or complex
  cancellation theorem would be a **mechanism upgrade**.
- Improving a witness floor changes a provider constant or rate unless it also
  changes how anchor entropy is paid.
- Discovering a new observability theorem for general hidden groups would be a
  mechanism-level extension.

Thus the present abstraction supplies a stable language for ranking future
work.
