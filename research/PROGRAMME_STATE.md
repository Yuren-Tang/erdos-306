```text

W_2    >= 4 H_2(R) D_*/Z^2.
```

The top prime block has

```text
D_*    asymp Z/log Z,

W_2    asymp H_2(R)/(Z log Z),
```

abandon worst-row absolute compression, use cancellation/adaptivity, exceed the
prefix ceiling, or decode low coordinates jointly.
